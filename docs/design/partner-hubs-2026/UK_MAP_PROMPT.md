# CURSOR PROMPT — The UK Music Hub Map

*Companion to `CURSOR_PROMPT.md`. Read that first; everything in it still applies. This prompt adds one signature feature: a whole-UK map that lets anyone zoom smoothly from the nation to their own hub and district.*

---

## 0. The brief in one sentence

Design and build a whole-UK map that feels like it came from a high-end design studio. It should be quiet, precise and tactile. A teacher should be able to go from "the UK" to "my district, my hub, my resources" in three gestures, without ever feeling lost.

The map is the front door to Partner Hubs. It is not a decoration, a gimmick or a dashboard widget. If a list would be faster for a task, the list must sit right beside the map and stay in sync with it.

---

## 1. What already exists — use it, don't reinvent it

- **`public/music-hubs/uk/uk-hub-map.json`** holds all geography and hub coverage. It is generated by `scripts/build-uk-hub-map.mjs`.
  - **Boundaries:** 361 UK local authority districts, 9 English regions and 4 nations, from ONS December 2024 ultra-generalised boundaries (Open Government Licence v3.0). The attribution must always be shown.
  - **Projection:** precomputed sinusoidal, centred on 2.5°W, in integer units of about 100 m. SVG paths are relative and ready to render.
  - **Hubs:** all 43 Arts Council England Music Hub areas, mapped district by district from ACE's published Hub Lead Organisations list. The build fails if any English district is unassigned or double-assigned.
  - **Per-hub fields:** `id`, `name`, `leadOrganisation`, `leadBody`, `authorities[]`, `lads[]`, `region`, `regions[]` (for cross-boundary hubs), `bbox`, `label`, `outline`, `verified`, `siteUrl`.
  - **Per-district fields:** `code`, `name`, `nation`, `region`, `hub`, `county`, `d`, `label`, `bbox`.
- **The existing Music Hubs tree** (`src/data/musicHubsDirectory.seed.json`, migrating to Supabase) owns page content, resources and publication status. The map owns geography. They join on hub `id` and district slug or ONS code. Never duplicate names or coverage in components.
- **There is no map library in the app**, and none should be added for this. A hand-built SVG renderer with viewBox animation is lighter, licence-clean, fully accessible and fully styleable. Do not add Leaflet, Mapbox, MapLibre, Google Maps or anything that needs tiles, keys or billing.

## 2. Zoom model — four levels, one continuous surface

| Level | What fills the frame | What's interactive | Labels shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UK** | All four nations; England tinted by its 9 regions | Nations and English regions | Nation and region names |
| **Region / nation** | One English region (or Wales, Scotland, NI) | Music Hubs in that region (whole-hub hit areas) | Hub names |
| **Hub** | One Music Hub's footprint, plus its neighbours faded | That hub's districts | District names |
| **District** | One district centred, the rest of the hub as context | The district; "Open [district]" call to action | District name and hub name |

- **Flights:** every level change is one animated camera move ("fly to") from the current view to the target bounding box, with padding.
  - Duration is 600–800 ms with a gentle ease-in-out (a cubic-bezier close to `0.22, 1, 0.36, 1`). There is no bounce or overshoot.
  - Interpolate in a way that feels like zooming, not sliding: centre and scale move together, and scale changes logarithmically.
  - Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, jump straight to the target (or cross-fade in under 150 ms).
- **Free navigation:** wheel and trackpad zoom around the cursor, pinch-zoom on touch, drag to pan, and double-click or double-tap to zoom in.
  - Clamp zoom between the whole UK and roughly district level × 4. Panning stops at the UK's bounds plus a margin.
- **Controls** (bottom-right, a small glass cluster): zoom in, zoom out and "Whole UK". Each is a 44 px target with an accessible name.
- **Step back:** a breadcrumb pinned top-left of the map (UK › East of England › Greater Essex › Chelmsford) — each crumb flies back to that level. Escape also steps back one level.
- **URLs:** every level is addressable, e.g. `/partner-hubs/map/england/east-of-england/greater-essex/chelmsford` or the existing route model with query params.
  - Refresh, deep links and the browser Back button must all restore the same camera.

## 3. Finding your place — search is a first-class citizen

- A floating search field sits top-left inside the map ("Find your area, hub or school town…"). On mobile it sits above the map.
- It searches districts, hubs, lead organisations, the upper-tier authorities each hub lists, and (later) postcode prefixes. Suggestions are grouped and labelled:
  - "Chelmsford — district · Greater Essex Music Hub"
  - "Greater Essex Music Hub — music hub · 14 areas"
  - "Norfolk Music Service — lead organisation"
- Choosing a result flies the map there and opens the matching panel. The list, map and URL all update together.
- It is fully keyboard-operable, following the combobox pattern with `aria-activedescendant`, arrow keys, Enter and Escape.

---

## 4. The side panel — the map's other half

- **Desktop (≥1024 px):** the map takes about two-thirds of the width and a calm panel takes the other third. The panel always describes the current level:
  - **UK:** a short intro, "Browse by region" (England's 9 regions with hub counts), and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, using their own terminology ("Music services and networks").
  - **Region:** the region name, a hub count and hub cards, each showing the hub name, lead organisation, areas covered and an "Explore hub" button.
  - **Hub:** the hub identity (name, "Lead organisation: …", the authorities covered), a two-column district list, "View all [hub] resources" and the official website. If the hub has been formally confirmed as a contributor, show that status too.
  - **District:** the district name, the hub it belongs to, a resource count *only if real*, "Explore [district]", and "See all [hub] resources".
- **Mobile:** the map comes first at about 60vh. The panel becomes a bottom sheet with three snap points (peek / half / full), dragged by a visible grabber. The list is always one tap away.
- **Sync:** hovering or focusing an item in the panel highlights the same shape on the map, and the reverse. Selection uses a stronger, consistent style in both places.

---

## 5. Visual language — "quiet cartography"

The reference point is fine printed cartography and premium product design, not a GIS tool.

- **Sea and background:** warm off-white (`#F7F8F6` to `#F3F6F3`, the CCD sage), with a very faint radial lift behind the UK. No blue oceans, no grid, no compass rose.
- **Land:** CCD sage-mist `#E8F0EA` at UK level, with region tints as gentle steps of the same green family. Every hub must differ in tone from its neighbours (use graph colouring over adjacency), but all tones stay within one restrained family.
- **Borders:**
  - Nation and region borders are 1–1.25 px white hairlines (non-scaling-stroke).
  - Hub outlines are 1 px forest green at about 35% opacity, coming forward at hub level.
  - District borders only appear from hub level, as 0.75 px white.
- **States:**
  - Hover: the tone lifts one step, plus a soft 2 px inner outline in teal `#14B8A6`.
  - Selected: deep forest `#002D24` fill with white label, or teal with forest label where contrast requires it.
  - Focus-visible: a 2 px teal ring, offset with a white halo, so it's always visible.
- **Partner colour:** a hub with a confirmed brand colour (e.g. EMS purple `#330968`) may use it *only* for the selected state and the panel header at hub level. It must never recolour the national map.
- **Typography:** the app font (Inter; Aptos if it's introduced app-wide).
  - Map labels use small caps or medium weight with slight tracking. Use a white halo (`paint-order: stroke`) rather than boxes.
  - Labels scale inversely with zoom so they stay 12–15 px on screen.
  - Avoid collisions: hide lower-priority labels instead of overlapping them.
- **Depth:** one soft shadow under the UK landmass at about 4% opacity and a very slight elevation on the selected shape. Nothing else.
- **Motion:** camera flights as above, plus 120 ms colour transitions. No pulsing pins, bouncing markers, auto-tours or parallax.
- **Attribution:** small, legible, bottom-left: "Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2024 · ONS (OGL v3) · Hub areas: Arts Council England". It must not be hidden behind a toggle.

## 6. Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

- Hub coverage comes only from the ACE list. Where ACE listed a lead as "TBC" (London – South West, South Yorkshire), show "Lead organisation: check Arts Council England" until a primary source is verified and recorded.
- **Status is separate from geography.** A hub shown on the map is *not* a CCDesigner partner.
  - Badges such as "Working example", "Listed" and "Confirmed contributor" come from data, never from styling choices.
  - Discussions with prospective partners do not authorise logos or partnership claims.
- **Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland:** show local authorities and the correct national framing (e.g. the National Music Service for Wales; local authority instrumental music services in Scotland; the Education Authority Music Service in Northern Ireland). Only name organisations whose role has been verified. Never present placeholders as providers.
- **Cross-boundary hubs** (BLMK, which includes Milton Keynes in the South East; Solent and BCP, which span the South West and South East) appear in every region they touch, with a one-line explanation.
- **No invented numbers.** Resource counts, teacher counts and "active schools" appear only when the database holds them.

---

## 7. Accessibility — the map must be fully usable without the map

- Every interactive shape has `role="button"` (or is a link where it navigates), an accessible name ("Chelmsford, Greater Essex Music Hub, 3 resources"), a tab stop *only at the current level* and Enter/Space activation.
- The side panel list is a complete equivalent of the map. A screen-reader or keyboard user should never need the SVG.
- Announce camera changes through a polite live region ("Showing East of England: 5 Music Hubs").
- Never use colour alone to show meaning. Text contrast must meet WCAG 2.2 AA; non-text contrast for boundaries and focus must be at least 3:1.
- Touch targets must be at least 44 px. Small districts get enlarged invisible hit areas at district level, and the list is always there as a fallback.

---

## 8. Performance and engineering

- Load the JSON lazily when the map first enters the viewport. Show a skeleton silhouette of the UK (inline, under 2 KB) while it loads.
- Render one `<svg>` with stable `<path>` elements and animate the `viewBox`. Use `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` for crisp hairlines at any zoom.
  - Batch hover with `requestAnimationFrame` and avoid React re-rendering 361 paths on every frame: keep the camera in a ref and write the `viewBox` directly.
- **Level of detail:**
  - At UK level, render nations and regions (about 40 KB of paths).
  - Fade in hub outlines when zoomed into England.
  - Mount district paths only for the visible region or hub.
- Target 60 fps on a mid-range laptop and smooth flights on a 2020 iPad. Measure in a performance trace and report it.
- Keep the projection and data pipeline in the build script. Components never parse GeoJSON at runtime.

---

## 9. Integration with the rest of the brief

- The map lives at the top of **Partner Hubs › Music Hubs** as "Browse by location", replacing the nested country accordions. The accordions' content becomes the side panel.
- **Region pages:** the East of England page opens the map pre-zoomed to the region.
- **Hub pages:** the Greater Essex page opens it pre-zoomed to the hub's 14 areas (12 Essex districts plus Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock). This makes it the same component as the national map, not a second map.
- **District pages:** a district selection leads to the reusable district template, with resources first and "Add to my library" wired to the existing Activity Library, Lesson Builder and Planner flows.
- The current `EssexDistrictMap` stays in place until the new map passes every acceptance test, then it is retired in a separate, reversible commit.

## 10. Acceptance tests (show screenshots and a short report)

1. **Find Chelmsford by map:** from the whole UK, three clicks (East of England › Greater Essex › Chelmsford) open the Chelmsford panel, then "Explore Chelmsford" opens the district page. Back returns to the hub view with the same camera.
2. **Find Chelmsford by search:** typing "chelm" and pressing Enter flies to Chelmsford and updates the URL. Refresh restores the view.
3. **East of England** shows exactly five hubs: Greater Essex, Hertfordshire, Cambridgeshire & Peterborough, Norfolk & Suffolk, and BLMK. BLMK carries its Milton Keynes note.
4. **Greater Essex** shows 14 selectable areas on the map *and* in the list, with the same names, slugs and order rules.
5. **Keyboard only:** Tab into the map, use arrow keys or Tab to move between hubs, Enter to zoom in, Escape to step back. Focus is always visible.
6. **Screen reader:** VoiceOver (Safari) and NVDA (Firefox) announce level changes and every hub and district name.
7. **Reduced motion:** there are no camera flights, and the map still works.
8. **Breakpoints:** 1440, 1280, 1024, 768 and 390 px screenshots show no overflow, clipped labels or dead space. On mobile the bottom sheet snaps correctly.
9. **Performance:** a trace of a UK → district flight shows no long tasks over 50 ms.
10. **Honesty:** no hub is shown as a partner unless the data says so, and attribution is visible at every level.

Work in Stage 1 (preview branch, screenshots, approval) before changing any production page.
