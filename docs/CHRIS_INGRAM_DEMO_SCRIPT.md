## Full VO (read straight through)

> Chris — this is Creative Curriculum Designer. It helps teachers plan performing-arts lessons, and it gives Music Hubs and arts organisations a way to put their resources in teachers’ hands — with evidence of use. First, The Facts: our public briefing on creative education in England.
>
> Teachers build from activities into lessons, then units and a calendar. Drag and drop, year groups, the whole planning loop — free to start, packs when they need specialist content.
>
> Partner Hubs sit beside the planner. Premium partners sell schemes; free organisations — LSO, ROH, OmniMusic, Jazz North, National Theatre, Tate — open as branded hubs with official classroom links and downloads.
>
> UK Music Hubs are browsable by country and region. Essex Music Service is featured; districts open on the map. Hub leads get their own public page and an admin side for content.
>
> Settings is where the school is configured: year groups, categories, objectives, resource shop. Teachers see My Downloads. Organisation accounts get download analytics. Admins manage users and roles — teacher, organisation, hub admin, super admin.
>
> Hub admins edit drafts, submit for approval; super admins approve and publish — so live pages stay controlled. Forum and registration sit on top for community. That’s the stack: plan, partner, prove. Happy to go deeper on any layer.

**~280 words** · speak ~140 wpm → lands under **2:00**.
