# Community guidelines (DRAFT — FOR OWNER REVIEW)

> **Status:** Draft only. Not binding until reviewed by the product owner / safeguarding lead.

## Purpose

The CCDesigner community forum supports arts educators sharing practice, planning ideas and product help.

## Expected behaviour

- Be professional and respectful.
- Do not post pupil personal data (names, photos, identifiable work) without appropriate consent and school policy compliance.
- Credit sources; do not upload copyrighted materials you lack rights to share.
- Keep announcements categories for official posts; general discussion belongs in the relevant topic category.

## Safeguarding

- Use the **safeguarding** report reason for child protection or urgent safety concerns.
- Safeguarding reports are prioritised separately from ordinary moderation (spam / off-topic).
- If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services — do not rely on the forum alone.

## Moderation

Moderators may hide, lock, pin or move content, and suspend forum posting privileges. Repeated spam, harassment or policy breaches may lead to account restrictions.

## Visibility

- **Public** categories may be indexable when published.
- **Members**, **hub**, **role-restricted** and **draft** categories are not publicly indexable.
- Display names are shown; emails are never shown in the forum UI.
